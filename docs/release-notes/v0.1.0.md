First release of PESM Coaching Zone: live Football Life 2027 match stats on your phone, over your home Wi-Fi.

**Downloads**
- Windows: `pesm-coaching-zone-windows-x86_64.exe`
- Linux: `pesm-coaching-zone-linux-x86_64` (run `sudo setcap cap_sys_ptrace+ep <file>` once, see README)

**What's in it**
- Match, XI, Stats and Ticker tabs in the phone's browser, no app needed
- QR code or 4-digit code pairing, home network only
- Live ratings, goals with assists, cards, substitutions, xG, team stats and flow per 15 minutes
- Player names, numbers and positions from your own mod's database (`--game "<FL27 folder>"`)
- Vibration on goals and cards, light and dark theme
- `--demo` to try it without the game

**Supported:** `FL_2027.exe` 1.00 on Windows and Linux (Wine/Proton/Lutris).

Read-only: it never changes the game, its files or your saves. Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Konami or the Football Life team.
