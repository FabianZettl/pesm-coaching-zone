**Substitutions from your phone**, a ticker with more going on, and an XI tab that shows exactly who is on the pitch.

**Downloads**
- Windows: `pesm-coaching-zone-windows-x86_64.exe`
- Linux: `pesm-coaching-zone-linux-x86_64` (run `sudo setcap cap_sys_ptrace+ep <file>` again for the new file, see README)

**New**
- Make substitutions from the phone: tap one of your players, pick one of three suggested bench players (same position first) or open the whole bench, confirm. FL27 makes the change at the next stoppage, like a sub from the pause menu. `--no-subs` turns this off.
- The XI tab reads FL27's live line-up, so it shows the eleven players really on the pitch, also when you start the program mid-match.
- Ticker: penalty goals, free-kick goals, missed penalties and big chances (with the keeper who saved). "Everything" also shows corners, free kicks, offsides and shots wide.
- The demo match now lets you try substitutions too.

Writes only the substitution you ask for. Never changes the game's files or your saves. Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Konami or the Football Life team.
