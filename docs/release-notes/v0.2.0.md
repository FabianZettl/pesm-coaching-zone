The real line-up, a newspaper-style ticker, team colours and substitution ideas.

**Downloads**
- Windows: `pesm-coaching-zone-windows-x86_64.exe`
- Linux: `pesm-coaching-zone-linux-x86_64` (run `sudo setcap cap_sys_ptrace+ep <file>` again for the new file, see README)

**New**
- The XI tab reads FL27's live line-up: exactly the eleven on the pitch, in the roles they play. A sub takes over the role of the player he replaced. Also right when you start the program mid-match.
- Ticker in newspaper style: big cards for goals (scorer, assist, score), red cards, missed penalties and grouped substitutions. New events: penalty and free-kick goals, missed penalties, big chances with the keeper who saved. "Everything" adds corners, free kicks, offsides and shots wide.
- Sub ideas: tap one of your players and get the three best fits from your bench, or browse the whole bench. Make the change in the pause menu; the phone shows it as soon as it's requested.
- Team colours from your mod's kits.
- Vibration only on goals, cards and missed penalties.

**Coming next:** making the substitution directly from the phone.

Read-only: it never changes the game, its files or your saves. Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Konami or the Football Life team.
