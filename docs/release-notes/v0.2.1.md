**Player names without extracting anything.**

- `--game "<FL27 folder>"` now also finds the player database inside the game's and the mods' `.cpk` files. Before, it only worked with an extracted database in a sider `cpk.root` folder.
- `--pesdb` also accepts a `.cpk` file.
- If no database is found, the PC window says where it looked.

**Club crests and player photos.**

- The scoreboard and the goal cards show the real club crests, and the ticker, player details and sub ideas show player photos, where your game or mods have them.
- They come from `common/render/symbol/flag` and `.../player` (PNG or DDS): first your active sider `cpk.root` folders, then the game's `.cpk` files in `download` and `Data`, then your other livecpk folders and `SiderAddons/content`.
- No crest or photo? You see the shirt and the team letters as before.
- Linux: no more typing `setcap`. If the app may not read FL27, it asks for your password, sets the permission and restarts itself.
- Tip for Windows: write `--game "C:\Games\FL27"` without a backslash before the closing quote. The app now also copes with it.

**Downloads**
- Windows: `pesm-coaching-zone-windows-x86_64.exe`
- Linux: `pesm-coaching-zone-linux-x86_64` (run `sudo setcap cap_sys_ptrace+ep <file>` again for the new file)

Read-only: it never changes the game, its files or your saves.
