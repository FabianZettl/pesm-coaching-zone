**New line-up view.**

- The XI tab shows every player with photo, live rating and overall rating (estimated from the player's abilities, usually within 1-2 points of the game's value).
- The bench and the player details show photo and overall rating too.

Also in this version (from 0.2.1): player names straight from the game's `.cpk` files, club crests and player photos, team colours for every team, a fix for matches that were not found, and on Linux the app asks for your password itself instead of needing `setcap`.

**Downloads**
- Windows: `pesm-coaching-zone-windows-x86_64.exe`
- Linux: `pesm-coaching-zone-linux-x86_64`

Read-only: it never changes the game, its files or your saves.
